Fill this out with your series' own numbers before launch week, and
again after the first promo run. planning figures from the chapters are
restated in the boxes so you can work with a pencil; exact per-unit
math belongs to **kdp-profit-planner**.

## 2. The ladder as it stands

Number the rungs 1–5 and note each rung's job:

- [ ] Rung 1 (door, e-book book 1): $ ____ — job: ____
- [ ] Rung 2 (books 2..N): $ ____ — job: ____
- [ ] Rung 3 (e-book bundle): $ ____ — job: ____
- [ ] Rung 4 (print singles): $ ____ — job: ____
- [ ] Rung 5 (print bundle): $ ____ — job: ____

Band guard (e-books): door upside in the safe zone; bundle inside the
70% band or above ~$20 ([chapter 4](04-pricing-ladders.md)).

Price-change ripple: expect **1–3 days**; confirm the live price during

## 4. The 99-cent/is-it-worth-it boxes

Fill from your own dashboard, work it, and keep
[chapters 5 and 7](05-read-through-that-pays.md) beside you:

```text
baseline:        book 1 sells ____ units / month at $ ____
full-price earn: $ ____ per copy  (        % band × price)

promo plan:      move door rung to $ ____ for 1 week,
                 expect   ____× volume lift  →  ____ promo units

new readers      ____ promo units  −  ____ baseline-month units
                 ≈ ____ genuinely new readers
give away        new readers × (full-price earn − promo earn)
                 ≈ $ ____
earn back (following months)
     book 2:     new readers × ____% × $ ____  ≈ $ ____
     book 3:     book-2 buyers × ____% × $ ____ ≈ $ ____
     book 4:     book-3 buyers × ____% × $ ____ ≈ $ ____
     bundle:     new readers × ____% × $ ____  ≈ $ ____
earn-back total  ≈ $ ____
verdict          earn-back ÷ give-away = ____×
```

A homemade rule of thumb: about **1.5× or better** = run it again next
series book; **1.1×–1.5×** = it paid but barely — check the read-through
plumbing before running again; under **1.1×** = not this series. Numbers
that stay under **1.0×** for two runs mean the door doesn't fill the
ladder: try a *bundle* discount or a book-2 door instead.
