# Beyond One Book (KDP)

**A series-and-boxset manual for authors planning on Amazon KDP — in plain
English, step by step, never condescending.**

You published your first book. Now someone asks the question that turns a
hobby into a business: *"Is the next one part of it?"* Once the answer is
yes, a whole second layer of decisions shows up that a single book never
made you think about:

- How does a **series page** actually get built, and why did your books
  end up in the wrong order on it?
- Can a **boxset** of three 400-page books physically exist as a paperback?
- What should each book cost, versus the bundle, versus the print edition?
- Does a **99-cent week** on book one ever pay for itself?
- And is this series even right for **Kindle Unlimited**, or should it go
  wide?

That's what this manual is for. It covers the series-level layer only —
what changes when a book becomes one of several. The per-book layer and
the non-Amazon layer are covered by companion guides (see below), on
purpose, so nothing here repeats itself.

## Who this is for

You've published — or you're drafting — more than one connected book, and
you want to make the series decisions *before* they cost you money. You
don't need to be technical. Every chapter works the same way: the idea,
the round-number arithmetic, then a checklist or worksheet you can fill
in on paper.

## Companion guides (deliberately not repeated here)

This manual is one part of a small local-first toolkit. The split is
simple:

- **kdp-profit-planner** — the exact *per-book* math: page counts by trim
  size, printing cost, royalty per unit at any price, break-even units,
  price scans, monthly projections. When this manual says "use a planning
  tool for your exact numbers," that's the tool.
- **publishing-beyond-kdp-guide** — everything *past* Amazon: which rights
  you keep, ISBN decisions, IngramSpark and other channels, libraries,
  audiobook routes, direct sales, and author taxes. Chapter 8 here only
  rounds off the wide-vs-KU *series decision*; the channel setup lives
  there.
- **book-keyword-lab-cli** — keyword-slot craft. Chapter 6 here covers
  only how intents split across books and the omnibus.
- **kdp-interior-templates** and **kdp-cover-bleed-checker-cli** — the
  print files and cover art that chapter 2 assembles into a bundle.

## The one paragraph of honesty

Every figure in this manual is a **round planning number** chosen to make
the arithmetic easy to follow — royalty bands, words per page, print
costs, page-read payouts. Real numbers move, and KDP's rules change (page
limits, payouts, promo cycles), so treat every table here as a first
sketch and verify in your own KDP dashboard, against the current KDP help
pages, before you price or publish. This is publishing strategy and
arithmetic only: it is not legal, accounting, or tax advice, and it is
not affiliated with Amazon. No data, accounts, or network calls are
involved — this guide is plain files plus a local build script.
