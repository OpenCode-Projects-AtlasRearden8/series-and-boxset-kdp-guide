# The glossary

KDP vocabulary in plain English, one line each, with a pointer to the
chapter where it matters. (The cross-store terms — ISBNs, wholesale
discounts, aggregators — live in the glossary of
**publishing-beyond-kdp-guide** and aren't re-defined here.)

| Term | Plain English | Where it matters |
|------|---------------|------------------|
| ASIN | Amazon's 10-character product ID; every product gets one automatically | [1](01-series-page-setup.md) |
| Back matter | the pages after the story ends: buy links, newsletter ask, about-the-author | [5](05-read-through-that-pays.md) |
| Band (royalty band) | the price range where the e-book royalty rate switches (70% band: $2.99–$9.99 on the US store) | [4](04-pricing-ladders.md) |
| Baseline sales | what a book sells on an average week with no promo — the denominator for lift | [7](07-launch-calendar-99-cent-math.md) |
| Bundle / boxset / omnibus | one product containing several published books; "bundle" = e-book habit, "boxset" = print habit, "omnibus" = everything | [2](02-boxset-build-steps.md) |
| Dead zone (prices) | e-book prices above $9.99 where 35% pays less than $9.99's 70% — until ~$20 catches up | [4](04-pricing-ladders.md) |
| Divider page | the page that restarts book 3 inside a bundle; what readers flip to find | [2](02-boxset-build-steps.md) |
| Door / door rung | book 1 (or a bundle) priced to be cheap to try; the ladder's first rung | [4](04-pricing-ladders.md) |
| Door price | the low price you run the door rung at during a promo week ($0.99–$1.99) | [7](07-launch-calendar-99-cent-math.md) |
| KDP | Kindle Direct Publishing — Amazon's self-publishing platform | everywhere |
| KENP | Kindle Edition Normalized Pages — what KU pays a per-page rate on, page by page | [8](08-wide-vs-ku-round-numbers.md) |
| Kindle Unlimited (KU) | Amazon's subscription: readers borrow freely; you're paid in page reads; e-book exclusivity applies per title, 90 days at a time | [8](08-wide-vs-ku-round-numbers.md) |
| Lift (volume lift) | how many times baseline sales a promo week does — a 6× week sells 6 baseline-weeks' worth | [7](07-launch-calendar-99-cent-math.md) |
| Ladder | your series' set of prices, arranged so each rung has a reader it fits | [4](04-pricing-ladders.md) |
| Metadata | the fields you fill at upload (title, series name/number, keywords, categories) — the series page is assembled from it | [1](01-series-page-setup.md) |
| Omnibus | a bundle of a *whole* series (or a big share of it), usually priced below the singles' sum | [2](02-boxset-build-steps.md), [4](04-pricing-ladders.md) |
| Pay-back (discount) | how much downstream read-through margin a discounted sale generates versus the margin discounted away | [5](05-read-through-that-pays.md), [7](07-launch-calendar-99-cent-math.md) |
| Permafree | a book kept free permanently on some stores — a wide-market pattern (see publishing-beyond-kdp-guide) | [8](08-wide-vs-ku-round-numbers.md) |
| Preorder | a listing that takes payment before release day; the standard tool for sequencing a series launch | [7](07-launch-calendar-99-cent-math.md) |
| Print cost | what KDP charges per printed copy (round-number: ~$0.85 + ~$0.012 per page, US) | [3](03-boxset-trim-math.md) |
| Read-through | percent of readers moving from book N to book N+1; the compounding engine of a series | [5](05-read-through-that-pays.md) |
| Series page | the store page grouping your books by series name + number | [1](01-series-page-setup.md) |
| Spine width | cover-art breadth of the spine, ≈ 0.0025 inches per page on cream paper | [3](03-boxset-trim-math.md) |
| Trim size | the physical page dimensions of a print book; wider paper = fewer pages | [3](03-boxset-trim-math.md) |
| Words per page | rough manuscript-to-paged-pages density by trim; plan with the low figure | [3](03-boxset-trim-math.md) |

*This glossary is one line per term on purpose. If a term feels wrong,
the "where it matters" chapter will slow down and work it with numbers.*
